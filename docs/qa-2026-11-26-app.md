# app.crea8.one QA — 2026-11-26

Scope: the logged-in app at app.crea8.one (not the crea8.one waitlist site), on desktop (1440px), tablet (768–1180px) and phone (390px). Read-only pass: nothing was saved, published, scheduled, deleted or invited. No JavaScript errors were seen on any page.

Not app bugs (test sandbox only): channel avatars from `pub-*.r2.dev`, Google favicons on Link-in-Bio links, and Sentry were blocked by the sandbox network, so those images look broken in screenshots.

## Fix first

1. **Tablet calendar grid is invisible.** From 768 to 1180px, Month, Week and Day render the grid at 2px high. The Channels panel drops to the bottom right and the rest is empty. In List view the Channels panel overlaps the tabs and "Page 1 of 1". 1280px and phone are fine. Look at the 640–1199px rules in `apps/frontend/src/app/c8-calendar.scss` (around lines 1838 and 3375).
2. **Link-in-Bio "Search and sharing" settings do nothing on the public page.** `/@crea8.one` has title `crea8.one`, `noindex, nofollow`, and no og:title, description or image. `SITE_STEALTH` (on by default) replaces the customer's page metadata in `apps/frontend/src/components/link-in-bio/link.load.ts:86`. Shared links get no preview card.
3. **Phone has no "Configure daily digest".** Stalker → Alerts shows only the toggle at 390px.
4. **Desktop "Add block" popover is clipped.** The right column reads "Featur post", "Heade", "Instagr post", "Contac details".
5. **Logged-out `/pricing` redirects to `/login`.** Sitemap and robots list it as public. Add `/pricing` to the public paths in `apps/frontend/src/proxy.ts`.
6. **Clicking a published post opens "Edit post"** with Update, Save as draft and Delete post together. "Save as draft" on a published post is unclear, and Delete sits next to the primary actions.

## Minor
- Every dashboard tab title is just "crea8.one".
- `launches/page.tsx` requires the `auth` cookie, while the proxy also accepts an `auth` header. With header only, the page loops client-side redirects (~40/s). Real users have the cookie, but the two checks should agree.
